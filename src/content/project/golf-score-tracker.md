A comprehensive golf tracking application built for golfers who want to improve their game:

- **Score Tracking**: Easy-to-use interface for recording rounds and scores
- **Handicap Calculation**: Automatic USGA handicap calculation
- **Performance Analytics**: Charts and graphs showing improvement over time
- **Course Database**: Integration with golf course information and ratings
- **Progressive Web App**: Installable on mobile devices with offline support

Personal project inspired by my love for golf, featuring intuitive design and powerful analytics to help golfers track and improve their performance.
